FAQ

Fair
questions.

Is this therapy?

No. Coaching is forward-looking and practical. If you’re struggling with your mental health, please speak to a licensed professional. I’m happy to work alongside one.

How much time does it take?

One 60-minute call a week, plus about 20 minutes a day on small experiments. Most clients say it gives time back by week four.

Do I have to be fully burned out?

No. Most people join while they’re still functioning. That’s the best time to start.

What if it doesn’t work for me?

We’ll review progress together at week four. If it’s not a fit, we’ll say so and part as friends.

Can my company pay for it?

Often, yes. I’ll send you an invoice and a one-page summary for your manager or L&D team.
