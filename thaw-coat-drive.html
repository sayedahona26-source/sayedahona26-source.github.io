Questions people ask before they give.

Is my gift tax-deductible?

Thaw is a registered nonprofit and every gift gets an emailed receipt for your records. Check with your tax advisor about your own situation.

What does the match mean?

Until Oct 31, neighbors at Larkspur Hardware match every gift up to $10,000 in total. So $40 becomes $80, which is two coats.

Do you take donated coats?

For this drive we buy new coats in bulk, so every kid gets the right size and a coat nobody else has worn. If you’d rather give time, volunteers are always welcome on fitting days.

Can I cancel a monthly gift?

Any time, with one email or one click in your receipt. No forms and no phone calls.

Will you show the kids’ faces?

Never. We don’t post photos of the kids we serve. You’ll get a note from the school instead.

Can my team or class give together?

Yes. Email hello@thaw.example with your goal and we’ll send you a shareable page and a thank-you for the whole group.
