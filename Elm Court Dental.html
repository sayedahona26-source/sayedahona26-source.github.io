What your first visit looks like.

  1. We talk first.

    Before any tools come out, you tell us what’s worrying you. We listen, then explain what we’d do.

  2. We take a look.

    A gentle exam. We only take X-rays if we need them, and we’ll tell you why.

  3. You see what we see.

    We show you photos of your teeth on a screen, so you know exactly what we’re pointing at.

  4. You choose the plan.

    Options and prices in writing. Nothing starts until you say yes.

Questions people are shy to ask.

Will it hurt?

We numb thoroughly before any treatment that could be uncomfortable, and we check in as we go. If you feel anything you don’t like, raise your hand and we’ll stop and adjust.

It’s been years. Will you tell me off?

No. You’ll get an honest look at where things stand and a plan that moves at your pace. Most of what we do on a first visit is listen.

How much will it cost?

You’ll see the cost in writing before any treatment begins. If you have dental cover, we’ll help you work out what it pays for.

Can I bring my kids?

Ask us when you call and we’ll find times that suit the whole family.

What if it’s an emergency?

Call us as early as you can. We keep space for emergencies and see people the same day wherever possible. If you have swelling in your face or neck, or trouble breathing or swallowing, go to the nearest emergency room right away.
