The book

Some debts are
paid in tides.

Skerra Island has had a lighthouse for three hundred years and a keeper for every one of them. When Ines Calloway arrives to take the post, she finds a village that will not speak her predecessor’s name, a lamp that burns without oil, and a wall of stopped clocks, each marked with a name and a date.

One of those names is her mother’s. The date is tomorrow.

As the nine tides of the autumn equinox rise, Ines must decide what she is willing to trade for the light, and what the sea will take in exchange.
